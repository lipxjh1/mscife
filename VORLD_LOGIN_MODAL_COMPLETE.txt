🔄 COMPLETE FLOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BEFORE (SAI):
  1. Click button
  2. Get email/password from main form (trống)
  3. Call RequestVorldLogin(empty, empty)
  4. ❌ Error: "Email must not be empty"
  5. User cannot login

AFTER (ĐÚNG):
  1. Click button
  2. EventBus.emit('show-vorld-login-popup')
  3. ✅ Modal appears with inputs
  4. User enters email + password
  5. Validation checks
  6. EventBus.emit('vorld-login-submit', {email, password})
  7. RequestVorldLogin(email, password)
  8. Backend validates
  9. ✅ OTP modal appears
  10. User enters OTP
  11. ✅ Login success
